Our Story

Built in Mysore.
Deployed worldwide.

Founded in the cultural capital of Karnataka, Atreya SoftTech was born from a belief that world-class software doesn't need to come from Silicon Valley. It needs to come from engineers who care deeply about their craft.

We combine the discipline of enterprise engineering with the agility of a product studio. Our team brings together specialists in cloud architecture, machine learning, design systems, and domain-specific business logic — all working in close partnership with our clients.

Every engagement starts with listening. We spend time understanding your industry, your constraints, and your ambitions before writing a single line of code.
